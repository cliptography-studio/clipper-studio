About Cliptography Studio

Cliptography Studio is a video content management and publishing platform. It combines structured video workflows with content intelligence, channel management, scheduling, and authorized publishing integrations.

The platform is designed so users can prepare video content, review generated clips and metadata, organize content for different channels, schedule publication, and connect supported social platforms through their official authorization flows.

TikTok Integration

When a user chooses to connect TikTok, Cliptography Studio uses TikTok's official authorization flow. The connected account is used only for features and permissions configured and approved for the application.

Account authorization: Authentication and consent take place on TikTok. Cliptography Studio does not ask users to provide their TikTok password.

For information about connected-account data, see the Privacy Policy. Service conditions are described in the Terms of Service.

Privacy & Security

Cliptography Studio is designed to process information needed for the features users choose to use. Platform authorization credentials are handled as part of the authorized integration, and connected-account information is handled according to the Privacy Policy.
