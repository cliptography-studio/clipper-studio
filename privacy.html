Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Clipper Studio ("Clipper Studio", "we", "us", or "our") is a video content management and publishing platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and process information when you use Clipper Studio and its supported social media integrations.

By using Clipper Studio, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Clipper Studio may process the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you connect a supported social media account, we may receive limited information necessary to identify and operate the connected account.

This may include:

  • Account identifier
  • Display name
  • Username
  • Profile information made available through the authorized API
  • Access and authorization information

2.2 TikTok Information

When you connect TikTok to Clipper Studio, TikTok may provide information permitted by the scopes and permissions that you authorize.

Depending on the approved TikTok integration, this may include basic account information and information required to publish content on your behalf.

Clipper Studio only requests permissions that are necessary for the features being provided.

2.3 Video and Content Information

Clipper Studio may process video files, captions, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and other content that you voluntarily provide for processing or publishing.

2.4 Technical Information

We may process limited technical information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Device information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Application logs

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Clipper Studio may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing Clipper Studio functionality
  • Connecting supported social media accounts
  • Processing and managing video content
  • Publishing authorized content to supported platforms
  • Maintaining authentication and authorization sessions
  • Providing technical support
  • Detecting and preventing abuse or unauthorized activity
  • Troubleshooting application errors
  • Improving reliability and performance
  • Maintaining security

4. Social Media Integrations

Clipper Studio may integrate with third-party platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and other supported services.

When you connect a third-party account, that platform may process your information according to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

Clipper Studio does not control the privacy practices, policies, or security of third-party platforms.

5. TikTok Integration

Clipper Studio may use TikTok for Developers products and APIs, including Login Kit and Content Posting API, to provide authorized TikTok account connection and video publishing functionality.

When you choose to connect your TikTok account, you will be redirected to TikTok to authenticate and authorize the requested permissions.

Clipper Studio does not request or collect your TikTok password.

TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok's official authorization mechanisms.

6. Access Tokens and Authorization

When you authorize a social media integration, Clipper Studio may receive access credentials such as access tokens and, where applicable, refresh tokens.

These credentials are used solely to provide the functionality you have authorized, such as maintaining the connection to your account or publishing content on your behalf.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect authentication credentials from unauthorized access.

7. Data Storage

Information may be stored temporarily or persistently when necessary to provide Clipper Studio functionality.

The type and duration of storage may depend on the feature being used.

Video files may be stored temporarily during processing, uploading, publishing, or other operations required by the service.

8. Data Sharing

Clipper Studio does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide a feature that you have explicitly requested or authorized.

For example, when you choose to publish a video to TikTok, the relevant content and information required by TikTok's API may be transmitted to TikTok.

9. Third-Party Services

Clipper Studio may rely on third-party infrastructure, hosting, authentication, storage, analytics, or social media APIs to provide the service.

Such third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where reasonably practical.

12. Disconnecting Social Media Accounts

You may disconnect an integrated social media account through available Clipper Studio functionality where supported.

Disconnecting an account may stop future API access but may not automatically delete information that is required to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

13. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Clipper Studio, subject to applicable legal, operational, and security requirements.

To request deletion of your information, contact us using the contact information provided below.

14. Children's Privacy

Clipper Studio is not intended for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

15. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Clipper Studio may use cookies, local storage, session mechanisms, or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, preferences, or application functionality.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Clipper Studio, our integrations, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
